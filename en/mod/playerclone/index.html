Player Clone

“Create player clones and unleash epic battles. Fight against yourself.”

🌌 Overview

Have you ever wondered what it would be like to fight alongside yourself… or against yourself? Player Clone brings that fantasy to life. With just a few simple commands, you can summon clones of any player and throw them into epic battles — free-for-all or team-based. Perfect for custom PvP arenas, strategy testing, or just chaotic fun.

Your greatest enemy… might just be yourself.

⚙️ Features

  • Instant Cloning: Create clones of any player with a single command.
  • Dynamic Battles: Launch Free-For-All (FFA) or team-based combat modes.
  • Full Control: Start, stop, or remove clones at any time.
  • Multiplayer Support: Works seamlessly in multiplayer environments.
  • Perfect for YouTubers & Mapmakers: Ideal for recording, events, or AI combat testing.

🧠 Mechanics & Commands

Main Commands

CommandDescription
/playerclone <username>Creates a single clone of the specified player.
/playerclone <username> <amount>Creates multiple clones of that player.
/playerclone <username> removeRemoves all clones with the given username.
/playerclone ffaStarts a Free-For-All battle among all clones.
/playerclone teamStarts a Team Battle where each username fights as a group.
/playerclone stopStops the current battle.
/playerclone helpDisplays all available commands.

🎮 How to Use

  1. Install Forge for your compatible Minecraft version.
  2. Place the .jar file into your mods folder.
  3. Launch your world or server.
  4. Use /playerclone commands to create, manage, and remove clones.
  5. Enjoy battling against your own army or leading them to victory.

🧩 Compatibility

  • Compatible with Minecraft 1.19.2, 1.20.1, and 1.21.
  • Works in singleplayer and multiplayer modes.
  • No additional dependencies required.
