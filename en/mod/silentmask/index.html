Silent Mask

“A mask that makes you invisible to monsters. Silence, tension, and pure stealth.”

🌌 Overview

Silent Mask is a Minecraft mod that adds stealth mechanics inspired by Five Nights at Freddy’s. When you wear the special mask, hostile mobs completely ignore you — as if you don’t exist. Perfect for horror maps, stealth gameplay, or FNaF-inspired experiences.

“Put it on… and they’ll think you’re one of them.”

⚙️ Features

  • Stealth Mode: All hostile mobs stop targeting you when the mask is equipped.
  • Target Reset: Clears mob aggression if they had already detected you.
  • Total Immunity: Walk freely among monsters without being attacked.
  • Immersive Overlay: Adds a visual mask effect with reduced field of view for realism.
  • FNaF-Inspired Aesthetics: Dark tones, eerie atmosphere, and emotional tension.

🧠 Mechanics & Behavior

When Equipped

  • All hostile mobs lose their current target.
  • They cannot re-target the player while the mask remains equipped.
  • A first-person overlay simulates wearing a real mask, restricting visibility for immersion.

Environment Interaction

  • Works in all biomes and lighting conditions.
  • May not affect mobs from mods with custom AI systems.

🎮 How to Use

  1. Install Forge 1.19.2.
  2. Place the .jar file in your mods folder.
  3. Launch the game and find the mask under the Tools tab in Creative Mode.
  4. Equip it in the helmet slot to activate stealth mode.
