Tung Tung Dweller

“An entity that lurks in the dark and feeds on fear. If you ever see it… it’s already too late.”

🌌 Overview

The Tung Tung Dweller is an environmental horror entity inspired by the Brainrot – Tung Tung Saur phenomenon. It emerges only when night falls… and the moment you dare to look directly at it, it awakens. Its haunting metallic “Tung Tung” sound marks the end of silence — a blend of psychological horror and pure suspense.

“At night you’ll hear it… during the day, you’ll feel it’s still there.”

⚙️ Features

  • Visual & Audio Horror: activates only when seen.
  • Fast, deadly reaction: once spotted, it releases the “Tung Tung” sound and rushes the player.
  • Instant disappearance: vanishes right after attacking, leaving no trace.
  • Daytime passive form: the Tung Tung Sahur appears during daylight, distorted and harmless, but ever-present.
  • Immersive tension: environmental sounds and visual effects keep you constantly on edge.

🧠 Mechanics & Behavior

Night Activation

  • Appears only at night.
  • Remains perfectly still and silent until eye contact is made.
  • Upon being seen, it emits its signature “Tung Tung” sound, charges toward the player, strikes once, and disappears instantly.
  • Leaves behind no drops or signs — only the echo of its presence.

Daytime Variant: Tung Tung Sahur

  • During the day, a passive version may spawn.
  • It does not attack but emits distorted ambient sounds that grow in intensity.
  • Slowly fades away as its echo dies out, leaving the world eerily quiet again.

🎮 How to Use

  1. Install Forge 1.19.2 and Geckolib.
  2. Place the .jar file in your mods folder.
  3. Create or load a world, then use /summon tungtung:tungtung_dweller to bring it forth.
  4. Wait for nightfall… and try not to look too long.

🧩 Compatibility

  • Compatible with Minecraft 1.19.2.
  • Requires Geckolib for model and animations.
  • No additional mods required.
