Omedweller

“A loyal companion with a dark side. Forget to feed him, and his monstrous form will awaken.”

🌌 Overview

Omedweller introduces a dual creature: the loyal Omebuddy and his terrifying alter ego, the Omedweller, who emerges when hunger takes over. Inspired by content creator Omenaso, this mod blends companionship and horror into one dynamic emotional experience.

A story of loyalty, friendship… and fear — all within a single mod.

⚙️ Features

  • Dual Entity System: Switches between Omebuddy (friendly) and Omedweller (hostile).
  • Emotional AI: Behavior changes depending on mood and hunger.
  • Combat Assistance: Can wield a sword and fight alongside you.
  • Block Destruction: The Omedweller breaks blocks while chasing you.
  • Advanced Animations: Powered by Geckolib for smooth and detailed motion.
  • Transformation Items: Use an Ometeddy to trigger a controlled transformation.

🧠 Mechanics & Behavior

Evolution

  • Omebuddy starts as your friendly companion.

    • Feed him two cookies a day — one in the morning and one in the evening.
    • If you forget, he transforms into Omedweller at noon or midnight.

  • Omedweller is a powerful, relentless creature.

    • Hunts down the player who neglected him.
    • Disappears after one minute or upon death, allowing Omebuddy to return.

Environment Interaction

  • Reacts differently based on the time of day.
  • Nighttime increases his ambient growls and tension.
  • Can destroy blocks in his path to reach the player.

🎮 How to Use

  1. Install Forge 1.19.2 and Geckolib.
  2. Place the .jar file into your mods folder.
  3. Start your world.
  4. Give him a sword and remember to feed him cookies regularly.
  5. If he transforms… you better run or fight.

🧩 Compatibility

  • Compatible with Minecraft 1.19.2.
  • Requires Geckolib for animations.
  • Works with EverMod Base, but can also run standalone.
